2. How it works — the decisions worth knowing

Lamps spawn on reveal, and that is what keeps them honest. An unrevealed stretch carries no lamps, so nothing glows through fog to hand you the network's shape — the same argument that made road reveal per-segment in the first place. Lamp cells are fixed in RebuildRoadCells, which generation and load both run, so the two can never disagree.

The stride counts from the road's start, not the segment's. Restarting per chunk would put two lamps a cell apart at one join and a double gap at the next — and the joins are frayed, so it would not even be consistently wrong. Lamp cells are filtered to cells the segment kept: a river can take the exact cell the stride landed on, and a lamp inside a river is a lamp underwater.

The two claim rules are deliberately opposite. A torch latches on ever-claimed and never goes out — a torch you lit is a thing you did. A road lamp tests current claim both ways, so losing a stretch relights it — a road you hold is a thing you are doing, and stopping should show.

Both polls live in TerrainFeatureGenerator, and that is a wiring decision before a cost one. It already owns reveal, the segment table and IsRoadSegmentHeld. A new floor component would need wiring onto the shared template prefab and the hand-placed Floor1Root — exactly the step that gets done on one and forgotten on the other. One accumulator at 1 Hz; a latched torch leaves its list for good.

Spawners never set tuning. ConfigureSpawned takes kind, colour and lit state, nothing else. Radius, target, halo and flicker stay on the prefab, where you can see them.
