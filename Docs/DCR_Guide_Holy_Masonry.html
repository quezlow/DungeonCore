3 · Fill the slots

Every slot takes either a (col, row) coordinate on the family sheet — column from the left, row from the top, both zero-based — or an override Sprite from any texture. Prefer coordinates: live thumbnails, bounds-checked, and the renderer sets the pivot itself (Center for caps and paving, Bottom for faces). Overrides are for mixing sources.

Minimum to see anything: cap mask 11 plus the Straight upper and lower faces. Every empty cap falls back to mask 11 and every empty face to Straight, so a half-filled family reads as plain masonry rather than as a hole. Fill 11 first, look at it, then widen.

Paving

Paving is painted on the floor tilemap, so it takes the floor's depth tint from FloorTint (a layer-wide multiply that cools and darkens with depth). Judge the colour on the floor the seal actually sits on, not on floor 0.

5 · Verify in game
If nothing changed at all, mask 11 is empty — present is false and the family is skipped as if absent. If the walls changed but the floor did not, the paving slots are empty. The two are independent by design.
