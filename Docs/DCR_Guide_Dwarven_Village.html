The offshoot ladder across the deep floors is deliberate and measured: 0 offshoots on index 2's lone trunk, 4 here (2 rim trunks + 2 broken spurs), 7 on index 4 -- the last living crossroads, already dying at its edges.

The village: a 41x41 walled hold placed AlongRoad with four 5-wide gates, quarters (Great Hall with hearth, two long-houses, the forge with coal stores, a terrace of three dwellings), a settlement name rolled per run from an 8-name roster, four static villagers, a Discovery alert, and two wisp lines. No vendor -- they trade at the gate, they live here.

Static on purpose. Villagers are the gatekeeper pattern: bare sprites, no pathfinding, no combat entity. The Living Holds arc (greenlit, next, fresh chat) makes them walk and brings patrols and caravans; villagerCount is the knob it grows.
