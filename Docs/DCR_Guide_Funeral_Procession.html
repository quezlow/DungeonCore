The Funeral Procession

Canon 50 · D2 Road Traffic, Stage 1 · they carry their dead down, not up
The delivery script has already applied every code edit and the canon entry. This guide is the manual half: three scene steps, the wisp regeneration, the tests, and the bier's art pointer. Precedence as always: canon > live code > roadmap. Prefix dcr-funeral-v1-.
0. What shipped, in one screen

When a road guard dies -- any killer, any floor -- the Deep Holds send a funeral two days later: three bearers and a bier, walking the caravan's outbound route one way, outpost to hold, carrying grave goods worth 40-100g. Deaths in the same window fold into one procession; a resolved journey holds the road quiet four days.

It is the caravan's toll machinery with one new verb. Click the sighted column and choose once: Desecrate (take the goods, standing -35, the survivors hurry on day and night), Tax (held stretch only, 20% min 1, no standing -- the toll economy's rule holds), Let pass, or Pay Respects (standing +2, live only when no carried death was the dungeon's -- mourning can never discount a murder).

No tier gate. The procession marches under an embargo -- burying the dead is the Holds' own affair, and the beat is that they do it in front of the power that made them. It does stand down at the climax, and waits while the hold lies fallen.

Everything is triggered, not rolled: this rides the relief cycle's pattern, never WorldEventDirector. The pilgrims (stage 2) are the framework rider.
