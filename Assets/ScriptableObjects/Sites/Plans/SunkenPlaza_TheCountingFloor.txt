@archetype: SunkenPlaza
@name: The Counting Floor
@doors: Marked

// A market plaza that was also a court: goods weighed, disputes settled,
// both on the same floor. The outer ring is open paving with pillar stubs
// and the surviving corners of a wall; the inner lip is the masonry edge
// of the sunken central square, with stair gaps mid-side.
//
// Junction-anchored, so roads arrive from several bearings. The plaza is
// mostly open floor and the carriageway subtraction passes through it
// harmlessly -- a road across a plaza is just more plaza.
